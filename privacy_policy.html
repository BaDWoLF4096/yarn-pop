Yarn Pop Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, score, boosters, daily rewards and sound settings are saved only on your device. Yarn Pop has no accounts and we do not run any servers that receive this information. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

Yarn Pop shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, device details, ad interactions and diagnostic data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, such as in the EEA and UK, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice in Settings under Privacy choices.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Google explains how it uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the game only whether the purchase was made.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only third parties involved are Google services, as described above.

Children

Yarn Pop is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Your choices

You can uninstall the game at any time to delete what is stored on your device. Data held by Google can be managed in your Google account and advertising ID settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the game. Questions: yarnpop.support@gmail.com
